# 🌄 Best Moment of Life - Trip with My Best Friends

This project is a small tribute to one of the most memorable trips of my life — a journey shared with my closest friends, full of adventure, laughter, and unforgettable moments. I built this website to capture those memories and preserve the spirit of that beautiful experience.

## 🖼️ About the Project

The website showcases a collage of photographs from our trip, with a focus on friendship, nature, and the joy of living in the moment. It acts as a digital photo album, reminding us of simpler times, stronger bonds, and the power of shared experiences.

### Key Features:

- 📸 A beautiful photo collage of the trip
- 🎨 Simple and clean layout
- 💖 Personal and emotional significance
- 📱 Responsive design (optional — if your site supports it)
